1. Plain-language summary

  • DriveWork is a work tool for drivers, vehicle owners and travel agencies in India — it keeps your trips, vehicles, documents, expenses, invoices and rate cards in one place.
  • We collect the information you type in or upload yourself: your profile, your vehicles, your documents, your trips, your expenses and your bills.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, SMS, call logs or microphone audio. The app asks for no such permission.
  • We run no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Your identity documents are stored privately and encrypted, and are readable only through short-lived links issued to you or to someone you have explicitly chosen to share them with.
  • You can delete your account and your documents from inside the app at any time (Settings → Privacy → Delete account).

3.2 Your profile

  • Driver profile: full name, mobile number, date of birth, state, city, and — if you signed up independently — email address, postal address and PIN code; the languages you speak, the vehicle categories you drive, and your work mode.
  • Agency / business profile: business name, owner name, contact person, mobile number, email address, GST number, company registration date, business address, city, state, PIN code, service areas, languages served, vehicle categories operated, declared fleet size, tagline, business logo, and a separate public contact number you choose to show to customers.

Why: to create your account, show the right screens for your role, let an agency verify a driver it is onboarding, and put correct legal details on the invoices you raise.

You may upload any of the following, together with the document’s own number and its issue and expiry dates:

  • Driving Licence
  • Aadhaar
  • PAN

  • Profile Photo

Why: identity and eligibility verification by the agency you work with, GST invoicing, and expiry reminders so a licence, insurance, PUC or permit does not lapse.

How they are handled: files are uploaded over HTTPS to a private cloud storage bucket (Amazon S3, Mumbai region) and are never publicly readable. Every read goes through a signed link that expires in 15 minutes. The maximum size per file is 10 MB.

Who can see them: only you, plus whoever you have explicitly allowed under Settings → Privacy → Document sharing, which has three settings you control — no documents shared, selected documents only, or all documents shared with managers. You can change or revoke this at any time.

3.4 On-device document scanning (no data leaves your phone)

When you add a photo or PDF of a document, the app reads the text on that image entirely on your device, using the operating system’s own on-device text recognition, so that it can pre-fill the document number and the dates for you instead of making you type them.

  • The image is not sent anywhere for this scan. There is no network call, no server, no third-party service and no API key involved in it.
  • The scan only suggests a value into an empty field. It never overwrites something you typed, and you can correct anything before saving.
  • Nothing from the scan is kept except the number and dates you choose to save with the document.

3.5 Vehicles

Registration number, category, make, model, fuel type, ownership/sourcing, vehicle preferences, and the vehicle photographs you add (front, back, sides, interior, odometer).

Why: to run your fleet records, attach the right vehicle to a trip, and price a job correctly.

3.6 Trips, bookings and routes

Trip and booking records, reference codes, trip type, pickup and drop addresses (and their coordinates where you enter or pick them), passenger count, distances, durations, your saved routes, dispatch and driver-assignment records, and the customer details you enter for a booking — customer name and contact number.

Why: to run the job, assign a driver and vehicle, calculate the fare, and raise a bill.

About navigation: when you tap to navigate, we hand your phone a plain Google Maps link and your Maps app opens it. We make no Maps API call, we do not send your position anywhere, and we do not ask for or receive a location permission for it — Google’s app starts from wherever your phone already is.

3.7 Money: expenses, pricing and invoices

  • Expenses: trip expenses and business expenses — amount, category, date, notes, and the receipt or bill photograph you attach.
  • Pricing: rate cards, vehicle rates, and the quotes generated from them.
  • Invoices: GST invoices and driver salary invoices, including the customer or driver details, line items, tax amounts and totals that appear on the bill.
  • Reports: expense and earning summaries derived from the above, cut by day, month and year in the India timezone (Asia/Kolkata).

Why: to give you your own books — what a trip cost, what to charge, what to pay, and what you earned — and to produce the tax-compliant documents you are legally required to issue.

3.8 Community, chat and shared rides

DriveWork has an in-app community for drivers and operators. If you use it, we process:

  • your community profile (display name, bio, avatar and cover images);
  • posts, comments, likes, shares, follows and the notifications they generate;
  • direct and group chat messages, and their delivery and read receipts, sent over a secure connection to our server;
  • rides you choose to share, hand over to another operator, and the settlement records for them.

Please note: anything you post to a community feed, or send in a group chat, is visible to the other people in that community or group. Treat it as you would any message you send to a group of colleagues, and do not post identity documents, customer phone numbers or bank details there. You can edit or delete your own posts, and you control who can see your community profile under Settings → Privacy → Profile visibility.

Chat messages are stored on our servers so they can be delivered and so you can read your history on a new device. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

3.9 Subscriptions and payments

DriveWork is a paid product with plans and a registration fee.

  • Payments are taken through Razorpay, and — where the app offers it — through Google Play Billing on Android or the App Store on iOS.
  • Your card number, UPI PIN, CVV, net-banking credentials and bank details are entered inside the payment provider’s own secure sheet. DriveWork never sees, receives or stores them.
  • What we do store is the outcome: the provider’s payment ID, order ID and signature, the amount, the currency, the plan, the status, the timestamps, and your invoice and billing history.

Why: to activate your plan, honour refunds and disputes, keep your billing history, and meet our own accounting and tax obligations.

3.10 Settings and preferences

Your language, appearance (theme), notification preferences (which kinds of updates you want, and your quiet hours), privacy choices, and the optional-access choices you make during setup.

Why: to make the app behave the way you asked it to.

3.11 Technical and security records

Server-side request logs, error records, authentication and refresh-token records, and the rate-limiting counters described in §3.1.

Why: to keep the service running, diagnose faults, and detect and prevent abuse, fraud and unauthorised access. These are operational security records, not a behavioural profile, and they are not used to build one.

4. What DriveWork does not collect or do

To be unambiguous, in this version of the app:

  • No location tracking. The app requests no location permission (neither precise nor approximate) and collects no GPS data, no background location and no location history.
  • No contacts, SMS, call logs or call recording. No such permission is requested.
  • No microphone or audio recording. No such permission is requested.
  • No broad file or photo-library access. You pick individual files with the system picker; the app never scans your gallery or storage.
  • No advertising. There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no ad SDKs and no ad profiling.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We integrate no third-party analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, advertising or social-media SDK whatsoever.
  • No social-media account connections. The app does not link to, read from, or post to Facebook, Instagram, X, WhatsApp or any other social platform.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, and no sharing of it with data brokers.
  • No automated decision-making that produces a legal or similarly significant effect on you. Approvals, rejections and verification decisions in the app are made by people — your agency, or our staff — not by an algorithm.
  • No credit scoring, no device fingerprinting, no reading of other apps’ data.

If any of this changes in a future version, we will update this policy, change the Data Safety declaration on Google Play, and ask for your consent where consent is what the law requires.

8. How we keep data safe

  • All traffic between the app and our servers travels over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Uploaded documents sit in a private bucket that is never publicly readable; each read uses a signed link that expires in 15 minutes.
  • Sign-in tokens are held in the device’s hardware-backed secure store (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) — never in ordinary app storage.
  • Access tokens are short-lived (30 minutes) and are refreshed; a refresh token lasts at most 30 days and is revoked immediately when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Passwords, where used, are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • OTP requests are rate-limited per phone number and per IP, with lockouts, so your number cannot be flooded and a code cannot be guessed.
  • Access to production data inside our organisation is role-based and restricted to staff who need it.
  • File uploads are size- and type-limited, and documents you replace are superseded rather than left lying around.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, and tell you what to do about it.

10. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — and, where they apply to you, the GDPR and other data-protection laws — you can:

  • Know and access what personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with;
  • Correct anything inaccurate, and complete anything incomplete — most of it you can edit yourself in the app;
  • Get a copy of your data. Use Settings → Privacy → Export my personal data, or email us. We verify your identity first, then send a copy of your profile, documents and trip records to the address on your account;
  • Delete your account and your data (see §11);
  • Withdraw consent you gave for something optional, at any time, in Settings — this does not undo what was lawfully done before you withdrew it;
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated, as the DPDP Act provides;
  • Complain — to us first (§13), and then to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

We answer rights requests within 30 days. We do not charge for them, and we never make you pay or trade anything to exercise one.

11. Deleting your account and your data

In the app: Settings → Privacy → Delete account. We confirm with you twice, and we verify your identity before acting.

By email: write to drivework.ai@gmail.com from the address or number on your account with the subject “Delete my account”, and we will do it for you.

What deletion actually does. The following is erased:

  • your name, mobile number and email address;
  • every sign-in method attached to your account (including the Google link) and every active session;
  • your date of birth, city and state;
  • every document you uploaded, and the stored file itself.

Your mobile number is released, so you can register again as a new account whenever you like.

What is kept, and why. Business records that other records depend on — vehicles, trips, orders, invoices and billing history — are retained in anonymised form, with your identity removed, so that an agency’s accounts and our own legally required tax and accounting records remain intact. Your name no longer appears on them.

Two things to know before you delete:

  1. Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel the subscription first — in Google Play (or the App Store) if you bought it there, or through the app’s billing screen — so you are not billed again.
  2. Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account, and content you posted in a community or sent in a chat may remain visible to others as part of their own conversation history.

Admin and staff accounts are managed by an administrator and cannot be self-deleted.
